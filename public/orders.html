Event invoice workflow

In-person pickup orders are invoiced the moment the shopper confirms, and they void automatically if not paid at the table within 30 minutes. Shipping requests wait for you to confirm the shipping cost and final total — they are never auto-expired. Inventory is deducted only when a paid order is marked Completed.
